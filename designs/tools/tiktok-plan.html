3. The three pinned videosPinned videos are the first thing a new visitor sees. These three explain the brand, start arguments in the comments, and show the shirts being read out loud.

Pin 1 · who we are

"We made shirts that say it for you"

  1. Hook text on screen in the first second.
  2. Turn around in Lidda Sno. Read it out loud.
  3. Quick cuts: Firs Koffee, then Lidda Sun.
  4. End: "Which one are you wearing?"

7–12 seconds. Tells anyone landing on the page what GiggleMe is.

Pin 2 · comment bait

"Lidda Sno or Lidda Sun?"

  1. Two people back to back, one in each shirt.
  2. Both turn around on the beat.
  3. Text: "Winter you or summer you?"
  4. Reply to every comment for the first hour.

Easy-answer questions pull comments, and comments push videos further.

Pin 3 · the reaction

"Read it out loud. I dare you."

  1. Hand a friend the Firs Koffee shirt, back up.
  2. They read it out loud and try not to laugh.
  3. Keep the laugh. Don't cut it.
  4. End on the close-up of the print.

Real laughs are the proof the shirts are funny. This is the one to remake.

4. What made his channel growThe pattern from the Got Funny interview, applied to GiggleMe.

Start before the store opens

He posted for two weeks before launch, one video took off, and people were asking to buy before he could sell. Post now and send people to your sign-up page.

When one hits, remake it

He kept reposting versions of the video that worked, with new designs. Same format, same hook, new shirt.

Same video, three apps

Post every TikTok to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts too. Same work, three times the reach.

Pay to boost the winners only

When a video beats your normal views, put about $20 a day behind it with Spark Ads. Never boost a video that didn't already work for free.

Let comments write shirts

"Comment a saying, we print the best one" gives you free ideas and a reason to come back for the reveal.

Post every day

Aim for 1–3 short videos a day for the first month. Most won't hit. You only need one to.
