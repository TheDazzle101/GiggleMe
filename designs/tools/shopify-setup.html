What his store does that sells

From his interview. Each one is built into the steps below and marked From his store. My additions are marked Upgrade.

  • A free Shopify templateNo custom website. He picked a prebuilt theme and spent about $0 starting. 5:05
  • Selling before the site was liveVideos went up two weeks early, and people were asking how to order. 3:23
  • An email pop-up on the first visitEveryone who lands gets asked for their email. 23:52
  • Five-star reviews everywhereReviews build trust for people who don't know the brand yet. 24:10
  • Auto review request, 10% off for photosA few days after delivery, an email asks for a review. Photo or video reviews get 10% off the next order. 7:12
  • "You checked this out" email with a codeVisitors who looked at a shirt but didn't buy get a discount code. 23:35

  1. 1

    Create the store

    Account, plan and store details.

    From his store
    1. Go to shopify.com → Start free trial. Use your brand email, not your personal one.
    2. Store name: GiggleMe. Store currency: US dollar.
    3. When the trial ends, pick the Basic plan. It has everything here.
    1. Settings → General: store email = your brand email, sender email the same.
    2. Add your business address (customers don't see it, but taxes and payouts need it).
  2. 2

    Put the store behind a password with a sign-up

    Your TikToks go up before launch, and this page catches every email.

    From his store
    1. Online Store → Preferences → Password protection → turn on, set a password only you know.
    2. In the theme editor, open the Password page template. Add your logo and an email sign-up block, then paste the text below.
    Password page text
    The first drop is almost here.
Funny shirts that say it for you. Lidda Sno, Firs Koffee, Lidda Sun and more.
Get on the list and get 10% off launch day.

We hope to always Giggleyou Viciously.
    Put this page's address in your TikTok and Instagram bio until launch day.
  3. 3

    Pick a free theme and brand it

    Logo, colors and fonts only. Don't build anything custom.

    From his store
    1. Online Store → Themes. Keep the default free theme, or add Dawn from the free themes. Both are fast and clean on phones.
    2. Click Customize.
    1. Header → Logo: upload designs/brand/giggleme-wordmark.png. Set the logo width to about 160 px.
    2. Theme settings → Logo → Favicon: upload designs/brand/social/profile-picture-monogram.png.
    1. Theme settings → Colors. Use these:
    WhereColor
    Page background#F6F4F0 warm white, matches your product photos
    Text#141416
    Header, footer, announcement bar#141416 with white text
    Buttons#3050D0 royal blue, white text
    Sale badges, accents#B8955A matte gold
    1. Theme settings → Typography: a bold, plain font for headings (for example Work Sans, weight 700) and the same family for body text. Plain fonts let the shirts be the loud part.
  4. 4

    Add the shirts through Printful

    Products must come from Printful so orders print and ship themselves.

    From his store
    1. Shopify Apps → search Printful → Install → log in to Printful and approve.
    2. Printful → Settings → Billing: add a card. Printful charges per order, and your Shopify payout lands about a day later.
    1. Follow designs/series-01/SHOPIFY.md. It has the Printful settings, titles, descriptions, prices, tags and photo order for all three.
    2. Save the first one as a Printful product template, then reuse it for every new shirt.
    Add new designs as fast as you make them. He doesn't wait to see if one will sell. The store decides for him.

    Reviews that ask for themselves

    His best revenue driver: an automatic review request, with 10% off for a photo or video review.

    From his store
    1. Apps → search Judge.me → Install. The free plan covers review emails, photo reviews and star ratings.
    2. Add its star rating under the product title and its review block on product pages and the homepage.
    1. Judge.me → Settings → Review request: send 7 days after fulfillment. Printful takes a few days to print, then shipping, so this lands a few days after delivery, like his.
    2. Paste the email below.
    1. In Judge.me, turn on review coupons and set them to 10% off, for photo and video reviews only. This may need a paid Judge.me plan; it pays for itself once orders start.
    Review request email subject, then body
    Subject: Did it make anyone laugh yet?

  9. 9

    Pop-up and automatic emails

    The pop-up collects emails. The automations turn them into sales. Shopify does both for free.

    From his store
    1. Apps → install Shopify Forms (free) → Create form → Pop-up.
    2. Offer: 10% off the first order. Shopify makes the code and emails it.
    3. Show it after about 5 seconds, once per visitor.
    Pop-up text
    Wanna giggle?
Get 10% off your first shirt and first dibs on every Friday drop.
[Email] [Get my 10%]
No thanks, I'm already hilarious
    1. Marketing → Automations → Create automation. Start from Shopify's templates, then swap in the text below.
    2. Shopify Email is free for your first 10,000 emails a month.
    1 · Welcome sent right after the pop-up
    Subject: Here's your 10%. Use it viciously.

Welcome to GiggleMe. Your code: {{ discount code }}

Every shirt is one short saying printed big across the back. Start with the ones people can't stop reading out loud:
[Lidda Sno] [Firs Koffee] [Lidda Sun]

New drop every Friday. You'll hear about it first.
    2 · "You checked this out" browse abandonment, 1 hour
    Subject: You were looking at this one...

It's still here. It's still funny.
Here's 10% off to make it yours: {{ discount code }}

[Finish the joke]
    3 · Abandoned cart 1 hour, then 24 hours
    Subject: Your shirt is waiting by the door

You left this in your cart. It's printed just for you when you order, so it's ready when you are.

[Back to my cart]
    4 · Abandoned checkout 1 hour
    Subject: So close. Like, one click close.

You were one step from wearing the joke. Your checkout is saved.

[Finish checking out]
    Put the 10% code in the browse email only, as he does. A code in every email teaches people to wait for one.

    How do the shirts fit?
Unisex and true to size. Size up for a roomier fit. See the size chart.

What are they made of?
Bella+Canvas 3001: soft, lightweight, 100% combed ring-spun cotton on Black and Navy.

How long does it take?
2–5 business days to print, then 3–5 to ship in the US.

How do I wash it?
Inside out, cold water, hang dry or low heat. The print lasts longer that way.

Can I suggest a saying?
Please do. Comment it on our TikTok. If we print it, we'll tag you.

  11. 11

    Shipping, payments and taxes

    Make sure money comes in and orders reach Printful.

    1. Settings → Shipping and delivery → United States: Standard $4.99, and free over $60.
    2. Two shirts cost about $70, so free shipping nudges people to add a second one.
    Upgrade
    1. Settings → Payments → activate Shopify Payments (bank account and tax ID).
    2. Turn on Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay, and add PayPal.
    1. Settings → Taxes and duties → United States → collect sales tax in your home state. Shopify works out the rate per order.
    1. Settings → Domains → Buy new domain. It connects itself.
  12. 12

    Test, then launch

    One real test order before you open the doors.

    1. Buy one shirt yourself with a real card while the store is still behind the password.
    2. Check it shows up in Printful under Orders, and that the confirmation email looks right.
    3. That shirt is also your first sample to film in.
    1. Online Store → Preferences → turn off the password.
    2. Email everyone on the list: "It's live. Your 10% is waiting."
    3. Post the launch video and change your TikTok bio to the after-launch version.
