    Which printer?

    Printful · Printify · Gelato

    Printful prints in its own factories, so quality is the most consistent from order to order. Printify is cheaper but quality depends on which print partner you pick (Monster Digital and SwiftPOD are well rated). Gelato is solid and prints locally in many countries.

    Recommended: Printful

    Sources: Blue Cotton · T-Shirt Elephant · AS Colour 5026 specs · Printful hoodie guide · Merch Titans. Always order a sample before selling.

    1. Design here, then press Save print file (PNG). You get a 3600 × 4800 px PNG at 300 DPI with a clear background, which matches Printful's 12 × 16 in tee print area.
    2. Fix anything red or yellow in Print check first.
    3. In Printful: Stores → your store → Add product, then pick a shirt from the list above (Bella+Canvas 3001 is the safe default).
    4. Upload the PNG, keep it centered at the top of the print area, and don't stretch it. Printful will flag low quality if anything is too small.
    5. Pick shirt colors that match the shirt color you designed on. Make a second version for light shirts if your text is light.
    6. Use Save mockup (JPG) for quick social posts; use Printful's own mockups for the store photos.
    7. Order a sample of every new design before you promote it.

    Print file spec: Printful DTG file guide (12 × 16 in max for tees, 150–300 DPI, PNG with transparency, sRGB).
