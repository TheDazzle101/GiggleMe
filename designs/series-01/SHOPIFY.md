# Series 01 on Shopify: first two shirts

Everything you need to list **Lidda Sno** and **Firs Koffee**. Copy each box straight into Shopify.

The images are in each shirt's `shopify/` folder (2048 × 2048 store photos, plus a 4:5 feed ad and a 9:16 story ad). The print files are `logo-left-chest.png` (front) and `<shirt>/back-print.png` (back).

## 1. Make the product in Printful (this connects printing to your store)

Create the product in **Printful**, not in Shopify. Printful pushes it to Shopify and handles the printing and shipping of every order. A product made directly in Shopify won't get printed.

1. Printful → **Stores** → your Shopify store → **Add product**.
2. Pick **Unisex Staple T-Shirt | Bella + Canvas 3001**. It's the best-reviewed soft tee at Printful, and it offers the big 15 × 18 in back print.
3. **Front → Left chest:** upload `logo-left-chest.png` and set the width to **4 in**.
4. **Back:** choose the **15 × 18 in** back print area and upload the shirt's `back-print.png`. Keep it full size, centered side to side and at the top of the area.
   Printful offers 15 × 18 on sizes **L to 5XL** ([Printful help](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/18532732956828-What-should-I-know-about-the-standard-15-18-print-placement-for-DTG-products)). Check the S and M previews before you publish to see how Printful handles them.
5. Colors: **Black** and **Navy**. Sizes: **S to 3XL**.
6. Prices are in the listing below. Printful shows your profit per size as you type.
7. Paste the title and description from the listing below, then hit **Submit to store**.

## 2. Finish it in Shopify

Open **Products** → the new shirt:

1. **Media:** delete Printful's mockups if you like, then upload ours in the order shown. Add each image's alt text (click the image → **Add alt text**).
2. **Variant images:** set the Navy variants to `back-navy.jpg`.
3. **Product organization:** Type `T-Shirt`, Vendor `GiggleMe`, Collection `Series 01`, plus the tags below.
4. **Search engine listing** (bottom of the page → Edit): paste the page title, meta description and URL handle.
5. Set Status to **Active** and turn on the Online Store channel.

## Ads

Each `shopify/` folder also has:
- `ad-feed-4x5.jpg` (2160 × 2700) for Instagram and Facebook feed posts and ads
- `ad-story-9x16.jpg` (2160 × 3840) for Stories, Reels and TikTok covers

Order one of each shirt for yourself before running paid ads. Real photos of you or a friend wearing it beat any render, and you'll see the print quality firsthand.
